# Playbook options

Playbooks are temporally extended, stateful control options.

## A — transition / breakout

Initiate when Direction is positive, Transmission is improving or broad, Episode is onset/expansion and fragility is not blocked.

## B — retest continuation

Requires memory of a prior positive episode. An orderly pullback is eligible only when medium-horizon Direction remains positive and Transmission does not structurally fail.

## D — wait unresolved

Use when sign is unresolved, evidence is missing, or structural attention rises before directional selection.

## F — overextended / no chase

Use when extension, narrowing breadth, concentration or exhaustion make a new long unattractive.

Template thresholds such as fixed volume multiples or tranche sizes are not treated as validated facts. They must be calibrated on training windows and tested prospectively.
