# Vehicle and Torque Layer

Vehicle choice occurs after a decision-sufficient theme state. Candidates are
Cash, Theme ETF, Theme Basket, and an ETF plus risk-normalized torque overlay.

Torque is not a fixed story-stock replacement for an ETF. An overlay must earn
eligibility through conditional upside/downside asymmetry, state-conditioned
incremental return, gap/event risk, liquidity, and equal-risk sizing. High beta
alone is insufficient. A frozen candidate map may define what can be evaluated;
it does not predetermine the action.

The overlay remains disabled in the default project. It may enter only after a
same-date vehicle ablation demonstrates incremental net value without merely
amplifying downside or a single issuer event.
