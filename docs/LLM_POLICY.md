# LLM policy

The LLM is a renderer and analyst over structured evidence.

## Allowed

- explain a State Certificate;
- summarize observer agreement and conflict;
- draft a post-mortem;
- translate structured evidence into accessible language.

## Forbidden

- invent catalysts or institutional flows;
- infer bullish direction from attention alone;
- override blocked gates;
- silently change playbook state;
- select a high-torque stock because its story sounds compelling;
- hide missing observations.
