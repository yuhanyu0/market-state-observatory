# Research agenda

## 1. Observer disagreement as a market state

Does disagreement between structural attention, Direction and Transmission lead regime selection, volatility expansion or risk concentration?

## 2. Decision-sufficient market states

Can a compact state preserve action rankings relative to the full observation history?

## 3. Response modes versus semantic themes

Are dynamic loading patterns more stable and transferable than fixed theme labels?

## 4. Active observation

Which next probe first distinguishes between plausible market states, and when is waiting more valuable than acting?

## 5. Boundary-dependent state transport

Does a compact state preserve meaning across volatility regimes, theme classes and ETF/single-stock boundaries, or require a boundary-dependent transformation?

## 6. Conditional torque

Does a risk-normalized high-torque overlay add equal-risk incremental return only in selected Direction/Transmission/Episode states?
