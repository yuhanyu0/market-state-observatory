# Theme Radar Role

Theme Radar is an external structural-attention observer. It may communicate:

- a versioned observer estimate;
- attention, migration, and persistence interpretation;
- source/version/hash provenance; and
- uncertainty and invalidation.

It may not select the Observatory's candidate universe, infer signed Direction,
serve as a Transmission or Episode label, gate decision eligibility, choose a
playbook, or choose a vehicle. Rising attention can coexist with negative price
direction, narrow propagation, exhaustion, or no tradable response.

Any incremental value claim must be evaluated as a sixth external benchmark
after source-native Direction, Transmission, Episode, playbook holding, and
vehicle layers have been frozen and tested independently.
