# Glossary

- **Observation:** immutable point-in-time fact with lineage.
- **Observer:** estimator of one aspect of market state.
- **Evidence graph:** support, contradiction, provenance and invalidation relationships.
- **Response mode:** dynamic market reaction pattern over assets.
- **Direction:** signed future-return distribution estimate.
- **Transmission:** whether the move spreads broadly or remains narrow.
- **Episode:** lifecycle state of a response mode.
- **State Certificate:** structured decision boundary.
- **Playbook:** stateful action option.
- **Data Shadow:** capture-only prospective evidence.
- **Model Shadow:** paper-only prospective model evaluation.
- **Torque:** conditional high-sensitivity execution layer, not unconditional high beta.
