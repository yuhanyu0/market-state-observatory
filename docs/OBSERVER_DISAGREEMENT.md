# Observer Disagreement

Disagreement is retained as evidence. It is never averaged away into one score.
A conflict records the two observers, conflict type, severity, explanation, and
the next observation that could discriminate between candidate states.

Initial conflict classes are:

- rising structural attention with negative or unresolved Direction;
- positive Direction with narrow or negatively coherent Transmission;
- positive Direction late in an exhausted Episode; and
- positive Direction blocked by Fragility.

Conflict does not imply that one observer is defective. It often identifies a
transition, a boundary condition, or a missing observation. High-severity
conflict prevents a decision from being treated as sufficient until the named
probe arrives or the opportunity expires.
