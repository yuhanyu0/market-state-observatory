# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — repository and public contract

- standalone repository;
- schemas, docs and website;
- publication boundary and CI.

## Phase 1 — Data Shadow

- point-in-time provider adapters;
- 20 consecutive valid capture days;
- public-safe readiness cards.

## Phase 2 — Direction Model Shadow

- transparent baseline first;
- probability calibration and cost-aware evaluation;
- no Graph SSM activation without incremental value.

## Phase 3 — Transmission and Episode

- constituent breadth and propagation;
- change-point and lifecycle semantics;
- ablation against Direction only.

## Phase 4 — Playbook and vehicle control

- A/B/D/F stateful controller;
- ETF versus Basket;
- conditional equal-risk torque experiment.

## Phase 5 — Observatory and research releases

- public State Certificates;
- observer-disagreement studies;
- response-mode research API;
- paper-quality experimental reports.
